# CORE LESSON

The single greatest habit for success is spending the first 4 to 6 hours of every day doing uninterrupted work on the highest-leverage priority in your business. Founders must identify the one outcome that makes all other problems vanish and focus entirely on it while aggressively ignoring urgent but unimportant distractions.

# KEY QUOTES

- "I work uninterrupted for the first 4 to 6 hours of my day on the things that I think have highest leverage in the business."
- "What's the one thing that if it were true, all of my other problems would go away? And then I put all of my effort into making that one thing happen."
- "You have to draw lines in the sand or you will be spread too thin, you will work all day, and you will get nothing done."

# TACTICS AND NUMBERS

- Dedicated focus window: Work uninterrupted for the first 4 to 6 hours of the day.
- Prioritization filter: Ask "What's the one thing that if it were true, all of my other problems would go away?" and direct all effort into that single objective.
- Boundary setting: Draw "lines in the sand" to block out urgent but unimportant mini priorities that prevent deep work.

# CONTEXT

Alex Hormozi, founder of Acquisition.com, discusses the core daily work habit that has contributed most to his business success.

# HOW TO APPLY

- Reserve the first 4 to 6 hours of every workday strictly for deep focus, eliminating meetings, messages, and interruptions.
- Identify your single highest-leverage task—the one accomplishment that would eliminate or solve your other current operational problems—and work exclusively on it during your focus window.
- Establish strict boundaries against non-essential urgent tasks so you do not spend all day working while accomplishing nothing meaningful.
