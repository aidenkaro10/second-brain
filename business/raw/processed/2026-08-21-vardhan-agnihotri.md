# CORE LESSON

AI post-training relies on two main approaches: Supervised Fine Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL). SFT trains models to mimic actions and copy correct answers for specific tasks, while RL rewards agents through trial-and-error in an environment to build broader capabilities. Understanding these techniques demystifies how AI models learn and operate.

# KEY QUOTES

* "You can think of an AI as a human in every sense of the word."
* "SFT is like teaching a child to mimic and copying the right answer, but RL is like tough love where you drop your kid into a playground..."
* "RL is better for learning a general variety of things, whereas SFT is better for learning a very specific task."

# TACTICS AND NUMBERS

* **Supervised Fine Tuning (SFT)**: Mimicking actions and copying annotated task-specific dataset answers; best for learning specific tasks.
* **Reinforcement Learning (RL)**: Agent exploring an environment, taking actions, receiving rewards ($r_t$), and updating states ($s_t$); best for learning a general variety of skills.

# HOW TO APPLY

* Apply Supervised Fine Tuning (SFT) when developing AI features that require specific, predictable task execution based on clear target outputs.
* Utilize Reinforcement Learning (RL) when training models to handle dynamic, complex environments requiring generalized problem-solving.
* Communicate basic AI mechanics to users and stakeholders to alleviate misconceptions and foster tech adoption.
