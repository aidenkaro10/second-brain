# Founder Mindset

## Success without killing your spark

From a TikTok reacting to a Jensen Huang (Nvidia CEO) interview: asked if he'd want to be 20 again, Huang said no, because the earlier time was when he was happiest.

The takeaway the creator draws, verbatim:

> "You can go drink, you can go party, you can talk to all the girls you want, but just don't let it kill your spark."

And the point of success:

> "The whole point of trying to be a successful person is not just to be a happier person, but to also give back to people"

**The lesson:** the "cut off friends, lock yourself in a room, only work" grindset isn't required for success. Balance is allowed as long as it doesn't kill your drive. Success should also mean giving back, not superiority.

## Deep work on the one highest-leverage thing

From an Instagram reel with Alex Hormozi (founder, Acquisition.com) on the single greatest habit behind his success.

> "I work uninterrupted for the first 4 to 6 hours of my day on the things that I think have highest leverage in the business."

His prioritization filter, verbatim:

> "What's the one thing that if it were true, all of my other problems would go away? And then I put all of my effort into making that one thing happen."

And on why boundaries matter:

> "You have to draw lines in the sand or you will be spread too thin, you will work all day, and you will get nothing done."

**The lesson:** protect the first 4-6 hours of the day for deep, uninterrupted work on the single highest-leverage priority. Find the one outcome that would make all other problems disappear, and put all effort there. Draw hard boundaries against urgent-but-unimportant tasks, or you'll stay busy all day and finish nothing that matters.

**How to apply:**
- Block the first 4-6 hours of the workday for deep focus, no meetings or messages.
- Ask "what's the one thing that if true, makes my other problems go away?" and work only on that during the block.
- Set firm boundaries against low-value urgent tasks that eat the day without moving the business forward.
