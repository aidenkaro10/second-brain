# My Business Profile

This file is about MY business, not lessons from videos (those live in the function files like [[mindset]]). Updates automatically from chats.

## Company

- **Rekko**: AI search visibility for local businesses. The pitch: AI recommends whoever the internet talks about, so Rekko controls what goes in so the business is what comes back out when customers ask AI for a recommendation.
- Serves local/SMB service businesses. Early client base coming from dad's friends and referrals, run manual-ish at first.
- Status as of the July 2026 investor materials: built by one person, product exists, but the deck was explicit that Rekko does not exist yet as a proven company — every claim was labeled a design claim.
- Positioning vs competitors: everyone else leads with a free scan and sells a one-time fix (a consulting motion). Nobody's doing recurring automated delivery.
- Branding: REKKO wordmark, the double-K as an echo/soundwave, no gradients or orbs.

## Goals and plans

- Stack: currently n8n workflows on Railway, Claude API as the intelligence layer, Supabase as the client data store, one parameterized master workflow per automation rather than duplicates per client. Eventually move off this into a real code base.
- After getting base customer data: fine-tune models on it — client brand voice, tone, industry language, top-performing content — so output stops being generic AI.
- Longer term: move into ML properly.
- Near-term roadmap locked in: finish the build, onboard 3 paying clients (discounted fine, free not), run them manual-ish for weeks 2–6 while logging every failure and every step touched by hand (that log is the product spec), add 3–5 more at week 6 and raise the price outside the referral circle, pull 60-day numbers from the first three and turn them into case studies.
- New Year's launch: campaign for businesses to level up in 2027 — selling the done-for-you agency at full price into January demand, not the platform.
- Productize the steps no longer touched by hand in Q1–Q2 2027, with engineering help.
- Rule: no new direction until 10 clients have hit 60 days. Target: REKKO to 10 customers.
