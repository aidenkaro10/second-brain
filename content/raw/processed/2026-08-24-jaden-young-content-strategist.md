# CORE LESSON

Effective content creation relies fundamentally on understanding human psychology. If you can capture a viewer's attention, retain them until the end of a video, and prompt them to take action, you are practicing human psychology. Creators do not need a formal psychology degree, but they must clearly understand what their target audience likes, dislikes, cares about, stands for, and stands against, and build content around those insights.

# FRAMEWORKS AND STEPS

* **Audience Psychology Profile Criteria**:
  To build psychological connection and drive engagement, analyze your target audience across five core dimensions and create content tailored to them:
  1. What they **like**
  2. What they **don't like**
  3. What they are **interested in**
  4. What they **stand for**
  5. What they **stand against**

* **The Core Engagement Objective**:
  High-performing content succeeds psychologically by executing three actions:
  1. Getting the viewer to **stop** on the video.
  2. Retaining the viewer to **watch to the end**.
  3. Spurring the viewer to **take action** (commenting, initiating ManyChat flows, etc.).

# HOW TO APPLY

* Map out your target audience's psychological profile by writing down what they like, dislike, are interested in, stand for, and stand against.
* Plan your content topics and messaging specifically to align with or address those audience values and triggers.
* Structure every post with a strong hook to stop the scroll, visual and narrative pacing to hold viewers until the end, and a clear call to action.
