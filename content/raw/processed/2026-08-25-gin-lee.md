# CORE LESSON

To write scripts that maximize audience retention and viral potential, structure content around clear comparison hooks, tight narrative transitions, and a consistent sign-off slogan. Hooking viewers with comparisons, connecting story beats using cause-and-effect transitions ("but" vs "therefore"), and conditioning the audience with a recurring signature phrase keeps engagement high throughout the video.

# FRAMEWORKS AND STEPS

1. **Chapter 1: Compare**
   - Start videos by comparing two or more subjects to show which option is better (e.g., Smart Video Editor vs. Dumb Video Editor).
   - Serves as the fastest way to hook audience attention right away.

2. **Chapter 2: But vs. Therefore**
   - When writing script sentences, transition between ideas using either "but" or "therefore" logic.
   - **"But" transitions (Conflict/Contrast):** *However, On the other hand, Nevertheless, The problem is, On the contrary.*
   - **"Therefore" transitions (Resolution/Consequence):** *That is why, Thus, As a result, Because of that, That leads to.*

3. **Chapter 3: Slogan**
   - End every video with the same repeated key phrase/slogan (e.g., "God bless you.").
   - Conditions viewers to hold out until the very end of the video to hear the slogan, raising viewer retention rate.

- Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds by contrasting two opposing items, methods, or choices.
- Review video scripts sentence by sentence, ensuring every line connects with contrast ("but") or cause-and-effect ("therefore").
- Develop a concise signature phrase to speak at the end of every video to build brand recognition and extend watch time.
