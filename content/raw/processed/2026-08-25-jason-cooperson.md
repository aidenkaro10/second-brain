# CORE LESSON

You can set up an automated video editing system using Claude to handle end-to-end post-production starting from raw footage. The pipeline automatically cuts filler and dead space, generates motion graphics, inserts dynamic visuals, and burn in subtitles without manual editing.

# FRAMEWORKS AND STEPS

**The Pipeline (Raw → Finished)**

1. **Intake:** Copy raw video footage in.
2. **Rough Cut:** Transcribe audio and trim out filler/dead space.
3. **Graphics:** Auto-generate motion graphics on beat.
4. **Captions:** Generate on-beat burn-in subtitles at the bottom of the screen.
5. **Music:** Add a flat-bed background music track.
6. **Export:** Export to `final.mp4` (or hand the rough cut off to Premiere).
