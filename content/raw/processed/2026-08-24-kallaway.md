# CORE LESSON

Social media algorithms test new posts on an initial sample audience of around 200 viewers, consisting of existing followers and interested non-followers. Depending on how this sample group interacts with the content across three main engagement metrics, the algorithm decides to either scale reach, test another small sample, or stop distributing the video ("200 view jail"). 

# FRAMEWORKS AND STEPS

### Initial Sampling Distribution Framework

1. **Initial Sample Test (200 Viewers):** The video is distributed to a test pool of ~200 viewers (a mix of existing followers and targeted non-followers).
2. **Performance Evaluation & Branching:**
   - **Positive Data:** The algorithm expands reach by an order of magnitude (e.g., to ~2,000 viewers).
   - **Neutral Data:** The algorithm tests the video on another small sample group of ~200 viewers.
   - **Negative Data:** The algorithm stops recommending the video, trapping it in "200 view jail."

### The Algorithm's 3 Key Metrics

1. **Average Watch Time:** The average duration of time a viewer spends watching the video.
2. **Engagement Rate:** The percentage of total viewers who interact with the video (like, comment, share, save).
3. **Watch Time Session Share:** The percentage of a viewer's total session on the platform spent watching your videos.

- Engagement rate metric examples shown: 13.1%, 18.7%.
- Watch time session share metric examples shown: 26.6%, 34.7%.

# HOW TO APPLY

- **Increase initial retention:** Craft clear hooks and maintain high pacing to elevate your video's Average Watch Time during the initial 200-person sample test.
- **Encourage interactive actions:** Prompt viewers to like, comment, share, or save to boost your overall Engagement Rate metric.
- **Binge-worthy content structure:** Create connected content series or compelling hooks that keep viewers watching multiple videos from your profile to maximize Watch Time Session Share.
