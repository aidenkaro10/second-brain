# Calls to Action

## "Follow to keep up to date" + next-episode teaser

Seen across the @alexxubuilds daily-challenge series, closing every episode:

> "So follow to keep up to date on this journey and see you tomorrow!" (60-day kickoff video)
>
> "Follow to keep up to date on this journey, and see you for Day 2." (Day 1 video)
>
> "Follow to stay up to date and see you for Day 17!" (Day 16 video)
>
> "So follow to join me on my journey and see you for Day 3!" (Day 2 video, @alexxubytes — see [[hooks/question]] for a handle mismatch note on this series)

**Why it works:**
- Promises a continuing story, not a one-off video, so following feels like joining something already in progress.
- Naming the next installment ("Day 2," "Day 17") gives a specific reason to come back — closer to appointment viewing than a generic "follow for more."
- Paired with a handle overlay (@alexxubuilds) right as the CTA lands, removing friction between deciding to follow and doing it.
