# Curiosity-Gap Hooks

Hooks that open with a promise to reveal something or tease an intriguing subject — not a question ([[question]]) and not an attack on a belief ([[contrarian]]).

## Examples

### "AI's own version of a search engine" (TikTok, @alexxubuilds)

> **Spoken:** "let me show you how to give AI its own version of a search engine"
>
> **On-screen text:** "let me show you how to give AI its own version of a search engine"

**Why it works:**
- Promises a clear, high-value technical explanation within the first 3 seconds.
- "AI's own version of a search engine" is a plain-language reframe of RAG (retrieval augmented generation) — hooks viewers who'd scroll past the jargon term itself.
- Video is episode 3 of a series ("the context bottleneck"), so the hook doubles as a promise of ongoing value for anyone who follows.

### "Andrej Karpathy is kind of a mythical figure in AI" (TikTok, @alexxubuilds)

> **Spoken:** "Andrej Karpathy is kind of a mythical figure in AI."
>
> **On-screen text:** "Andrej", "of", "AI"

**Why it works:**
- Calling a real, respected figure "mythical" creates intrigue for viewers who don't know him and validates the video for viewers who do.
- Borrowed authority: the video's credibility rides on Karpathy's reputation before the creator has said anything about himself.
- Immediately backed up with a resume/tweet/thumbnail montage that proves the "mythical" claim.
