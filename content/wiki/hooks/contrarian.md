# Contrarian Hooks

Hooks that open with a bold claim against what the audience believes or has invested in.

## Examples

### "Coding is irrelevant" (TikTok, @swerikcodes)

> **Spoken:** "Honestly, I've been thinking that coding is irrelevant in 2026,"
>
> **On-screen text:** "22 Y/O CTO explains how to get a job in 2026" + "press here for 2x speed"

**Why it works:**
- Bold claim that coding degrees are obsolete instantly hooks tech-focused viewers. The target audience (CS students, developers) has years invested in exactly what the hook dismisses, so they can't scroll past.
- The on-screen text stacks credibility ("22 Y/O CTO") on top of the contrarian claim, answering "why should I listen to you" in the same 3 seconds.
- The video then pivots from the problem statement to an actionable roadmap, so the contrarian claim is a setup, not the whole video.
