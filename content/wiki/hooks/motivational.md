# Motivational Hooks

Hooks that open with a bold, quotable motivational line (not a claim against the audience's beliefs — a relatable push to take action).

## Examples

### "Slow motion is better than no motion" (TikTok, @alexeubank2-0)

> **Spoken:** "And slow motion is better than no motion. A lot of people choose to be complacent and they choose to not do anything because they get overwhelmed by what they know that they ought to do, so therefore they do nothing at all. At least some sort of motion and some sort of needle movers is better than none at all."
>
> **On-screen text:** word-by-word kinetic build — "slow" → "slow motion is better than" → "A lot of people choose to" → "complacent and they choose to" → "not do anything because they" → "get overwhelmed" → "that they, to do, so therefore they do nothing at all." → "At least SOME sort" → "and some sort of is better than"

**Why it works:**
- Opens with a short, quotable line ("slow motion is better than no motion") that works as a standalone caption/comment — easy to remember and repeat.
- The line reframes "doing a little" as good enough, which lowers the bar for the viewer to feel called out (they've been complacent) without feeling attacked.
- Delivers a single, concise takeaway in under 15 seconds instead of a multi-point roadmap.
