# Platform Tactics

## TikTok

### "Press here for 2x speed" text overlay

Seen in the @swerikcodes "coding is irrelevant" TikTok: a persistent top-of-screen overlay reading "*press here for 2x speed*".

**Why it works:** encourages viewer interaction (long-press to speed up) and completion rate. A viewer watching at 2x is far more likely to finish the video, and finishing boosts the video in the algorithm.

### Urgency close

Same video ends with: "because if you don't know this, you're simply going to get replaced in the next couple of years."

**Why it works:** a high-stakes warning as the final line drives comments and shares (fear + stakes), right at the moment the viewer decides whether to engage.
