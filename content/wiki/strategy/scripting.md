# Scripting

## Core lesson

Scripts that maximize retention are built, not improvised: open with a comparison to hook fast, connect every sentence with a "but" or "therefore" transition to keep pulling attention, and close every video with the same signature slogan to condition viewers to watch to the end.

> "Here's how I write scripts so they *almost* go viral every time."

## Framework: Compare, Transition, Slogan

1. **Compare** — open the video comparing two or more subjects to show which is better (e.g. Smart Video Editor vs. Dumb Video Editor). Fastest way to hook attention right away.
   > "Whenever you start a video, compare two or more subjects to show which is the better option."

2. **But vs. Therefore** — every script sentence transitions into the next using either "but" (conflict/contrast) or "therefore" (resolution/consequence) logic.
   - **"But" transitions:** However, On the other hand, Nevertheless, The problem is, On the contrary.
   - **"Therefore" transitions:** That is why, Thus, As a result, Because of that, That leads to.
   > "Whenever you write your next sentence, the transition should always be either 'but' or 'therefore'."
   > "Just by using 'but' or 'therefore' as your transition, you can constantly pull your audience's attention."

3. **Slogan** — end every video with the same repeated phrase (e.g. "God bless you."). Conditions viewers to wait for it, raising retention.
   > "By doing this, you can hypnotize your audience to wait until the end to hear the phrase, which means higher retention, and a higher chance of going viral."

## Numbers

- Smart vs. dumb video editor comparison video: **125K** views.
- Cinnamon roll vs. strawberries-and-chocolate-sauce comparison video: **16.6M** views.
- Retention curve shown stays high across a 0:30 timeline.

## How to apply

- Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds by contrasting two opposing items, methods, or choices.
- Go sentence by sentence in your script and make sure each line connects with "but" (contrast) or "therefore" (cause-and-effect) — no dangling, disconnected sentences.
- Pick one short signature phrase and say it at the end of every video, every time, to build recognition and stretch watch time.
