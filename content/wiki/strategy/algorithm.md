# Algorithm

## Core lesson

Social media algorithms test new posts on an initial sample audience of around 200 viewers (a mix of existing followers and interested non-followers). How that sample group reacts, across three key metrics, decides whether the algorithm scales the video's reach, tests it on another small sample, or stops distributing it entirely ("200 view jail").

> "When you post a video, the algorithm samples it with a small number of viewers first."

> "Remember, the algorithm's only job is to keep viewers on the platform as long as possible."

> "The reason you're not getting more views is because the algorithm doesn't want to risk more unhappy viewers."

> "If you can increase those 3 metrics within that first 200 person sample group, your videos will do much better."

## Framework: Initial Sampling Distribution

1. **Initial sample test (~200 viewers):** the video goes out to a test pool of existing followers and targeted non-followers.
2. **Performance evaluation and branching:**
   - **Positive data:** reach expands by an order of magnitude (e.g. to ~2,000 viewers).
   - **Neutral data:** the video gets tested on another small sample group of ~200 viewers.
   - **Negative data:** the algorithm stops recommending it — "200 view jail."

## Framework: The Algorithm's 3 Key Metrics

1. **Average watch time** — how long a viewer spends watching the video on average.
2. **Engagement rate** — % of total viewers who like, comment, share, or save.
3. **Watch time session share** — % of a viewer's total platform session spent watching your videos.

## Numbers

- 200 viewers: initial sample size algorithms use to gauge video performance.
- 2,000 viewers: expanded audience tier when the initial test data is positive.
- Engagement rate examples shown: 13.1%, 18.7%.
- Watch time session share examples shown: 26.6%, 34.7%.

## How to apply

- **Boost average watch time:** strong hooks and tight pacing to hold the first 200 sampled viewers.
- **Boost engagement rate:** prompt likes, comments, shares, saves directly in the video.
- **Boost watch time session share:** build connected series or compelling hooks that pull viewers into watching more videos from your profile in one sitting, so they spend more of their session on your content specifically.
