# Video Formats

## Talking head + rapid resource overlays (listicle)

Direct-to-camera talking head where every named tool or resource gets a quick graphic, logo, or screenshot popped over the video. Seen in the @swerikcodes "coding is irrelevant" TikTok: creator sits in a park, and as he names each resource the screen shows it (GitHub's "The System Design Primer" repo, AWS Cloud Practitioner site, Docker/Kubernetes logos, Amazon's MLU-EXPLAIN site).

**Structure of the example (0:54 total):**
1. 0:00-0:03 contrarian hook, direct to camera ([[hooks/contrarian]])
2. 0:03-0:17 problem: AI tools write code, your degree feels irrelevant
3. 0:17-0:45 rapid-fire roadmap: system design, then DevOps/cloud, then version control
4. 0:45-0:54 final resource + urgency close ("you're simply going to get replaced")

**Why it works:**
- High information density. Rapidly lists concrete, free learning resources to maximize perceived value and saves.
- Clear structure: problem statement first, then an actionable roadmap (System Design → DevOps → AI).
- Fast cuts between screenshots keep visual novelty high while one continuous take of audio carries the argument.

## Talking head + lifestyle-contrast b-roll

Short direct-to-camera bursts intercut with quick lifestyle b-roll (couple at sunset, drone shot of an estate, formal-wear poses) and grounded gym footage, all under kinetic on-screen captions. Seen in the @alexeubank2-0 "slow motion is better than no motion" TikTok.

**Structure of the example (0:13 total):**
1. 0:00-0:02 talking head, hook line begins ([[hooks/motivational]])
2. 0:02-0:07 fast-cut lifestyle b-roll (mansion, couple) while voiceover continues
3. 0:07-0:13 return to grounded gym footage (deadlift, workout) to close

**Why it works:**
- Blends aspirational lifestyle imagery with relatable gym effort to keep viewers visually engaged while the voiceover carries one continuous argument.
- Pace varies shot-to-shot (fast lifestyle cuts, then a held gym shot) so the edit itself mirrors the "motion vs. no motion" message.
