## Content plan

- Series decided: 30-day learn-in-public series (dropped the "100 days" framing). Hook: "no CS degree, no company, NYC didn't work out — building the skills anyway."
- Format habit being adopted from alexxubuilds: each post explains one concept clearly enough for a stranger to learn something — not diary/day-count content. Guardrails: only explain things actually built and gotten working (never things just read about), and say plainly when unsure to invite correction instead of criticism.
- Rollout plan: don't announce the 30-day series publicly on day 1. Post daily for two weeks first with no branding/day-count. If still going and there's real stuff to show by day 14, name the series and launch publicly with that progress already banked. If it fizzles early, no public cost.
- Lane locked in: building real apps with AI tooling and explaining what it actually generated (leans on Python + vibe-coding background, undersupplied niche vs. generic "learning to code").
- Earlier launch arc: Week 1 of philosophy/worldview videos about me → NYC/Jane Street AMP content for authority → then the build series.
- Content pillars: build, mindset, business insight — alternating, never two of the same in a row.
- Posting cadence: has done 3–4 videos/day Mon–Thu bursts, but posting more in a day sometimes kills views, so the working plan settled at roughly 1–2/day, spaced, with strongest videos in evening peak slots.
- Marathon content structured as a three-beat arc: hype, race day, reflection.
