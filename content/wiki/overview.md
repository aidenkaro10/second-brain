    "'You're an AI pro.' Said no one, ever. The uncomfortable truth is most people have no idea how AI actually works, and knowing how it works makes your outputs better.

    Day 3 of learning the skills to become an AI startup founder. Today: the transformer, the architecture behind basically every LLM out there.

    First, tokenization, breaking text into small pieces the model can actually read.

    Then embeddings, turning each piece into a list of numbers, vectors, that capture its meaning.

    Then positional encoding, stamping in the order those pieces came in, since the model reads them all at once, not left to right.

    Then self-attention, the model checking how every piece relates to every other piece in the sentence.

    All of that stacked together is the encoder, it reads and understands the input.

    Then the decoder takes over, generating the output one word at a time, using what the encoder understood plus everything it's already generated.
